Picking a Profile for Your Levy County Property

Homes in town: Standing seam gives a crisp finish; 5V crimp offers a traditional Florida look.

Manufactured homes: Lightweight metal roofing is a common reroof choice; the installer confirms the structure and method.

Farms and acreage: PBR is practical for barns and shops and can be color-matched to the house.

Toward Fanning Springs and the Suwannee: Humid, wooded sites benefit from coatings and fasteners suited to moisture; ask your installer.

Before You Sign: Questions for the Installer

Which profile works on my roof pitch? Will the old roof be removed? What underlayment and fastening schedule meet code here? Is the permit through the City of Chiefland or Levy County? How will vents, valleys and skylights be flashed? Ask for answers in writing.

Color Choices

White, light gray and tan reflect the most heat through a long Chiefland summer. Galvalume, barn red, forest green and charcoal suit rural homesites and outbuildings.
